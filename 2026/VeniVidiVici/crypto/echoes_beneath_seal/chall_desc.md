A secret message has fallen into your hands, carried by means best left unspoken. It speaks of something important being moved, carefully hidden behind layers of protection.

What appears at first glance to be a chaotic collection of fragments is no accident. Repetition has left subtle cracks for those patient enough to notice the pattern in the chaos.

Above it all lies a short, unreadable note. It is sealed by a simpler spell, yet standard counter-spells fail to break it. Perhaps the Ministry isn't using the standard runes we are used to?

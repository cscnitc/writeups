The Department of Mysteries has gone dark.

In the chaos following a botched raid on a forbidden archive, a Confundus Charm slipped past the wards and seeped into the Ministry’s deepest vault — the one that remembers everything. Memories fractured, records misaligned, and truth itself was scattered.

All that remains is a single recovered directory — thirty relics, pulled from the wreckage.

Most are harmless echoes. A few, however, were twisted by the charm — their essence rearranged, their true nature disguised.

Among these corrupted remnants:

    Some belong to the Hunt
    Some exist only to deceive

The fragments you seek no longer recognize themselves. Their beginnings lie out of order, their identities masked. Only by restoring what was once known and mixing them together using exclusive potion can the truth be reassembled.

Choose wisely. Not every memory wants to be remembered.

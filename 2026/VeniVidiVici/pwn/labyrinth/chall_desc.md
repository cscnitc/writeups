    "The Labyrinth remembers everything… and forgives nothing."

Deep within an ancient magical maze lies a prophecy-bound system that records every challenger who enters. Your identity is logged, your sanity measured, and your worthiness silently judged.

The Labyrinth is old magic — trusted, layered, and seemingly unbreakable. Records are meant to remain consistent. Identities are meant to stay intact. Those who fail its checks are cast out without mercy.

Yet legends tell of champions who altered fate itself — not by brute force, but by bending the rules of the ritual just enough to pass unseen.

Beyond the final trial waits a Dragon, sworn to obey destiny alone. Only those marked as worthy may face it — and only those who speak carefully will survive what follows.

The prophecy is watching.

For Utopia 1 the flag format was apoorvctf{artistName_diseaseName}. The challenge was to find the artist the conspiracy theorist JohnBuck69420 was talking about
and to find the name of the disease the artist was predicting

**JohnBuck69420** looked like a username so I searched all the popular socials and found@JohnBuck69420 on Twitter(X), a newly created account. 
The posts on the account helped verify that it was indeed related to the CTF.
